Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026. Your financial data is personal — here's how we handle it.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide Spendly to you: your email address and password (stored securely) when you register, and the expense records you choose to log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect financial account numbers, bank credentials, or any information beyond what you explicitly enter.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Service — to display your expenses, generate summaries, and let you filter and analyse your spending. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running Spendly. We will never sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your expense data is stored securely and is accessible only to you through your account. Passwords are hashed before storage and are never stored in plain text. You may request a full export or permanent deletion of your data at any time, and deletion requests are processed within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services strictly necessary to run the app — such as web fonts served by Google Fonts. These services may receive standard request metadata (such as your IP address) as part of normal web operation. We do not share your expense records, email address, or account details with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within a reasonable timeframe.
